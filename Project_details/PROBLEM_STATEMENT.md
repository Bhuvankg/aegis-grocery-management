# AEGIS AI – AI-Powered Grocery Store Management System

## Problem statement
Small grocery stores lack affordable and intelligent tools to manage inventory, track sales, and analyze business performance, resulting in operational inefficiencies, stock-related problems, and poor business decisions.

## Product boundary
Customers can browse products, inspect quality information, read reviews, add items to a cart and place orders. Customers cannot create or edit products, change prices, or access store analytics.

Only an authorized administrator can access product management, inventory, order management, sales and AI insights.
